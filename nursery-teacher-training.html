Program Overview

Why N.T.T?

The Mark of Excellence

The Quality Management System is in Compliance with ISO 9001:2008. Scope Of Certification includes Design, Development, and Conduct of Job Oriented and self employment Nursery Teacher Training Course.

Quality Policy

All India Early Childhood Care & Education is committed to impart Job Oriented & Self employment Nursery Teacher Training in Conformity to the requirement vis-à-vis sanctity and dignity of education to ensure maintenance of desired standards.

Objective

  • To provide women (graduates and under graduates) with the knowledge and skill which will enable them to run their home and guide their children more efficiently.
  • To provide vocational training which will help them get jobs in the middle level management.
  • To educate them about social needs of the society/country and to awaken in them a desire to serve.

Need of Training

The preschool age (2–5) is the most impressionable age in one’s life. The effective teacher therefore should be aware of the principle of child development and need of the children, which are different from the primary and post primary children. This is the reason we conduct training courses with utmost care giving the emphasis on child psychology of this age group. The demand for nursery school and early childhood care is increasing day by day; teachers who possess requisite qualification and training are in great demand.
