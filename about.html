Who We Are

Welcome To Junior Kidz

We are known to have a theme based & child-centric curriculum that help your child learn & acquire knowledge. Physical activities is also part of growth and development and that's how the kids here are growing. Reading with phonetics is one such method we follow at JuniorKidz. An innovative method for quick & easy learning. We have facilities like water pool & garden area where your child can play and stay active in all the extra curricular activities. We organize and conduct field trips, Sports day & Annual Day on regular intervals. Spacious and AC class rooms that create a healthy comfortable learning environment. They are designed in a way keeping in mind that they remain child friendly.

All our teachers and staff members are certified & have rich experience. To take extra care about the security. Thus, all our preschool premises are CCTV Enabled. We offer student kit that help your child in learning actively. Our passion to provide the young minds with the best education is what made us who we are and it is still the same as it's only the name that has changed but our mission, vision, passion and our team is still the same.

Our Horizon

Our Vision

A well-known educational institution, Junior Kidz Playgroup & Nursery Franchise in Mumbai was founded in the year 2010. The Schools takes pride in the holistic and innovative learning methods which have helped them raise the bar in the field of education.

The centre follows a well-researched curriculum that is based on the play way method of learning. The school offers education for children in different age groups. There are a host of other supplementary activities that children can enroll in to enhance their skill set.

Undoubtedly it is one of the best preschools franchise in Mumbai.
